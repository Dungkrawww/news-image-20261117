# news-image-20261117 — Tuesday 17 November 2026 in one image

One-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of Tuesday
17 November 2026: Ukraine hits the Kerch bridge again, tankers struck by
mines near Hormuz, Xi–Trump–Putin to meet Thursday in Shenzhen, a third US
carrier reaches the Middle East, the Pentagon fails its audit for the ninth
year, and Japan scrambles jets at Russian planes.

- `entry.png` — the image (1024×1024, drawn by `tools/make_image.py`)
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
- `tools/make_image.py` — draws the image with Pillow (Noto Sans), no network

Run: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`
